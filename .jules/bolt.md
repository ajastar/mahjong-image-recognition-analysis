
## 2024-05-18 - [Python Modulo-3 Suit Count Check for Mahjong Agari]
**Learning:** In CPython, evaluating `sum(1 for c in hand_34 if c >= 2)` or doing complete loops over 34 items to find the head for a standard mahjong hand introduces significant overhead when called thousands of times in PIMC simulations. By applying domain-specific mathematics—that a standard winning hand consists of 4 sets of 3 and 1 pair of 2, thus the sum of the suit containing the pair must be 2 (mod 3) and all other suits must be 0 (mod 3)—we can fast-fail over 90% of invalid hands and restrict the head search to a single suit without using Numba. Unrolling the sums directly (e.g., `cnt_m = hand_34[0] + hand_34[1] + ...`) rather than using slices and `sum()` provides an additional 30% speedup in raw Python.
**Action:** When working in tight simulation loops in raw Python, use domain-specific math (like modulo checks on subset sums) to fast-fail invalid states, and unroll array accumulations for small fixed sizes instead of using built-in slices/generators.
