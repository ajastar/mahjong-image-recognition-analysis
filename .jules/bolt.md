## 2024-05-24 - Domain-specific Math Optimizations in Mahjong AI
**Learning:** In Mahjong hand evaluation (`is_agari`), iterating through 34 potential tile candidates to find the "head" (pair) is extremely slow in both pure CPython and JIT-compiled Numba loops. A mathematically sound optimization applies modulo-3 arithmetic constraints to the sum of tiles in each suit. Because a standard winning hand consists of four sets of three and one pair of two, the suit containing the pair must mathematically sum to 2 (mod 3), and all other suits must sum to 0 (mod 3).
**Action:** Always look for domain-specific mathematical constraints to short-circuit loops before resorting to generic language-level micro-optimizations, as algorithmic pruning yields the most significant performance gains.
