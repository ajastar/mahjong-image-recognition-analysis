## 2024-06-11 - Generator and List Comp in Python Hot Paths
**Learning:** Generator comprehensions passed directly into `sum()` (e.g., `sum(1 for x in arr)`) introduce significant overhead in CPython due to generator function creation and iteration calls. Replacing them with list comprehensions inside `len()` (e.g., `len([x for x in arr])`) or explicit loops is substantially faster. Additionally, constantly re-creating dynamic static lists like `[0,8,9,17,18,26] + list(range(27,34))` incurs massive allocation overhead in loops; they should always be flattened into module-level constants (e.g., tuples).
**Action:** Always prefer `len(list_comprehension)` over `sum(generator_comprehension)` for counting, and hoist all dynamically generated static lists out of hot paths into module-level constants.
