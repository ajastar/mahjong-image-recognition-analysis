## 2024-07-28 - Optimize get_suji via LRU cache and tuples
**Learning:** Functions like `get_suji` are duplicated across multiple files and are called heavily in hot paths for risk/safety calculation. Returning a newly allocated `list` for each call is slow. Because `get_suji` relies entirely on a domain of 34 mahjong tiles, caching it via `@functools.lru_cache(maxsize=34)` and returning immutable `tuples` provides a significant and completely cache-safe performance boost. The lack of mutation in the downstream loops (`for s in get_suji(t)`) makes this completely safe.
**Action:** Applied `@functools.lru_cache(maxsize=34)` and modified the return type to `tuple` across `factory_main.py`, `Jantama_Attach_Mode.py`, `Background_Multi_Mahjong.py`, `single_bot.py`, and `mahjong_logic.py`, whilst injecting `import functools` to prevent NameError.
