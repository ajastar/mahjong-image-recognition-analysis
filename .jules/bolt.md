## 2024-05-18 - Modulo-3 Constraint for Fast Mahjong Agari Checking
**Learning:** Mahjong hands evaluate millions of invalid states during PIMC simulation loops. Because a valid standard hand consists of 4 sets of 3 and 1 pair of 2 (14 tiles total), the total tile count in the suit containing the pair must mathematically be 2 (mod 3), while all other suits must be 0 (mod 3). Using this domain-specific constraint along with unrolled summation avoids looping and slices, yielding a massive >3x performance gain by instantly fast-failing invalid hands and strictly bounding the head-search domain to just one suit.
**Action:** Always search for rigid mathematical properties (like modulo arithmetic) inside small-domain loops to prune invalid states early before running computationally expensive full-state evaluations.
