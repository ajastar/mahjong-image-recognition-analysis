## 2024-03-01 - Mahjong Agari Suit Modulo-3 Optimization
**Learning:** In highly recursive constraint-satisfaction problems (like checking Mahjong standard hand shapes), domain-specific mathematical invariants can drastically prune the search space. A valid standard Mahjong hand is exactly 4 sets of 3 and 1 pair of 2. Therefore, across any suit partition, the suit containing the pair must mathematically sum to 2 (mod 3), while all other suits must sum to 0 (mod 3). Without this invariant check, the code was needlessly attempting to brute-force a head (pair) candidate across all 34 tiles.
**Action:** Always look for mathematical invariant constraints (like modulo-sums) that can fast-fail early or restrict the search space in combinatorial problems, especially inside hot loops like PIMC simulations, rather than trying micro-optimizations inside the recursions.
