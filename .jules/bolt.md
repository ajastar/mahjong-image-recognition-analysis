## 2024-05-24 - Modulo-3 Pruning in Agari Checks
**Learning:** Found a major architectural bottleneck in Mahjong logic. Checking permutations without mathematical constraint creates overhead. Removing `any()` checks over small arrays was rejected as a trivial micro-optimization. However, standard Mahjong hands must consist of 4 sets of 3 and 1 set of 2. Calculating the sum modulo 3 of each suit identifies mathematically invalid hands in O(1) before attempting permutations, saving ~35% on pure python processing and ~40% under numba.
**Action:** When working on board/card games, look for invariant mathematical constraints (like parity or modulo checks) that allow the engine to fast-fail before diving into expensive recursive searches or permutation evaluations. Also, completely avoid submitting standalone `test_perf.py` scratch files when making PRs.
