## 2024-05-31 - Fast membership checking and unrolling loops in Python
**Learning:** Python overhead for list allocation and comprehensions inside of hot paths is unexpectedly large. Converting dynamically created lists to module-level `frozenset` improved membership lookups (`in`), and explicitly unrolling small fixed-length generator arrays (like `sum(hand_34[i] for i in [0,...])` into `hand_34[0] + ...`) resulted in roughly an 85% speedup due to avoiding frame allocation and `next()` calls entirely.
**Action:** Always extract invariant collections to module-level constants (especially using `frozenset` for membership checks), and unroll small aggregate loops in performance-critical methods rather than relying on Python comprehensions or the `sum()` built-in over generators.
